## Emblem icons

The glyphs in the middle of career challenge and feat emblems come from
[game-icons.net](https://game-icons.net), used through the `react-icons/gi`
package, under [CC BY 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). The
emblem medallions and crests around them are drawn by this project
(`packages/client/src/cosmetics/Emblem.tsx`).

The icons used are by **Lorc**, **Delapouite**, **Skoll** and **Carl Olsen**
(each icon's author is in the table below and on its game-icons.net page). The
Trophy cabinet tab names them in its footer. Authors were checked against the
upstream repository ([game-icons/icons](https://github.com/game-icons/icons),
one folder per author) by matching each `react-icons/gi` icon's path data;
where two authors have an icon of the same name, the table names the one
`react-icons` actually ships.
